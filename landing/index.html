How it works

Build a profile around where you're headed

Your current title, your dream title, your goals and a few prompts. Everything on it is written so someone can reply.

Connect on substance

Cards lead with goals and experience. Swipe right to connect, left to pass, or use the buttons if you'd rather tap.

It's a connection

When you both tap Connect, photos appear and the chat opens. We'll show you what you have in common.

Grab coffee

Every new chat starts with a gentle nudge toward a short coffee chat on campus, plus a couple of ways to break the ice.
